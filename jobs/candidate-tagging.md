# Candidate Tagging

## Purpose

Candidate Tagging allows recruiters to associate candidates with a specific job requirement and initiate the recruitment process.

Candidates can be tagged either through AI Candidate Discovery or by manually selecting candidates from the ATS candidate database.

Once tagged, candidates become part of the job-specific recruitment workflow and can proceed through screening, interviews, submissions, and placement activities.

---

## Intended Users

The Candidate Tagging feature is available to:

* Account Manager
* Delivery Manager
* Technical Recruiter

---

## Accessing Candidate Tagging

Candidate Tagging can be initiated from multiple locations within the Jobs module.

### Option 1: From Jobs List

```text
Jobs
    ↓
Tag Candidate (On each job's right pane)
```

---

### Option 2: From Job Details

```text
Jobs
    ↓
Open Job
    ↓
Tag Candidate
```

---

### Option 3: From Candidate Discovery

```text
Jobs
    ↓
Candidate Discovery (Enabled on each job's left pane )
    ↓
Tag Candidate
```

Recruiters can review AI-matched candidates and tag suitable profiles directly from the discovery workspace.

---

### Option 4: Bulk Job Selection

```text
Jobs
    ↓
Select Two or More Jobs
    ↓
Tag Candidate (On the top right pane)
```

This option allows recruiters to initiate candidate tagging from selected job records.

---

## Tag Candidate Popup

Selecting the Tag Candidate option opens the Tag Candidate popup window.

The popup captures candidate and recruitment-specific information before associating the candidate with the selected job.

---

## Candidate Information

### Select Candidate

Recruiters can search and select candidates from the ATS candidate database.

The dropdown displays available candidates along with their candidate identifiers.

Example:

```text
CID1026714 - Candidate Name
```

### Job Title

Displays the job title associated with the selected requirement.

This field is automatically populated based on the selected job.

---

## Availability Information

### Availability

Recruiters can specify candidate availability details based on the hiring requirement.

This information helps determine the candidate's readiness to join the project or organization.

---

## Relocation Preference

Recruiters can indicate whether the candidate is willing to relocate.

Available options:

* Yes
* No

---

## Pay Rate Information

The Pay Rate section captures candidate compensation expectations.

### Available Fields

* Currency
* Minimum Rate
* Maximum Rate
* Rate Type

This information helps evaluate candidate expectations against job budgets.

---

## Bill Rate Information

The Bill Rate section captures billing information associated with the candidate.

### Available Fields

* Currency
* Bill Rate Amount
* Rate Type

Bill rate information assists in financial planning and client billing activities.

---

## Engagement Mode

Recruiters can select the preferred engagement model for the candidate.

The available options are determined by system configuration and business requirements.

---

## Past Experience with Same Client

Recruiters can indicate whether the candidate has previously worked with the same client.

Available options:

* Yes
* No

This information may be useful during candidate evaluation and client discussions.

---

## Comments

The Comments section allows recruiters to capture additional information about the candidate.

Examples include:

* Candidate observations
* Recruitment notes
* Client-specific requirements
* Evaluation remarks

---

## Tag Candidate

After completing the required information:

1. Verify candidate details.
2. Review compensation information.
3. Add comments if required.
4. Click **Tag Candidate**.

The selected candidate is then associated with the job requirement.

---

## Outcome

After successful tagging:

* Candidate becomes associated with the selected job.
* Candidate becomes available in the job recruitment workflow.
* Recruiters can proceed with candidate evaluation and interview activities.
* Candidate becomes eligible for submission and placement processes.

---

## Benefits

Candidate Tagging helps recruiters:

* Associate candidates with job requirements quickly.
* Build job-specific talent pools.
* Track candidate-job relationships.
* Capture recruitment-related information.
* Prepare candidates for downstream hiring processes.
