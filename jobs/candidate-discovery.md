# Candidate Discovery

## Purpose

Candidate Discovery helps recruiters identify and evaluate AI-matched candidates for a specific job requirement.

The system analyzes the job description and automatically presents candidates from the ATS database based on their relevance to the selected job. Each candidate is assigned an AI matching score to help recruiters quickly identify suitable profiles.

---

## Intended Users

The Candidate Discovery feature is available to:

* Account Manager
* Delivery Manager
* Technical Recruiter

---

## Accessing Candidate Discovery

### Navigation Flow

```text
Login
    ↓
Select Business Unit
    ↓
Jobs
    ↓
Select Job
    ↓
Click Candidate Discovery (AI Icon)
```

Selecting the Candidate Discovery icon opens the AI candidate matching workspace for the selected job.

---

## AI Matching Candidates

After Candidate Discovery is launched, the system automatically analyzes the job requirement and displays matching candidates.

Each candidate profile includes:

* AI Matching Score
* Candidate Name
* Candidate ID
* Experience
* Current Location
* Key Skills
* Last Modified Date

The candidate list is ranked based on the AI matching score.

---

## Candidate Match Score

The AI score represents how closely a candidate's profile matches the job requirement.

Higher scores indicate stronger alignment with the required skills, experience, and job criteria.

Recruiters can use the score as an initial indicator while evaluating candidates.

---

## Candidate Actions

The following actions are available for each candidate profile.

### Candidate Summary

Click the Candidate Summary icon to view a consolidated summary of the candidate profile.

The summary may include:

* Skills
* Experience
* Specialization
* Professional Background
* Additional Candidate Information

This helps recruiters quickly evaluate candidates without opening the complete profile.

---

### View Resume

Click the Resume icon to open the candidate's resume.

Recruiters can review:

* Employment History
* Technical Skills
* Project Experience
* Education
* Certifications
* Additional Qualifications

---

### Tag Candidate

Click the Tag icon to associate the candidate with the selected job.

Tagging a candidate makes them available for the recruitment process and enables further activities such as:

* Candidate Review
* Interview Coordination
* Pipeline Management
* Candidate Submission

Tagged candidates become part of the job-specific recruitment workflow.

---

## Auto Shortlist Top 10

The Auto Shortlist Top 10 option automatically identifies and shortlists the highest-ranked candidates for the selected job.

This helps recruiters reduce manual screening effort and focus on the most relevant profiles.

---

## Bulk Actions

The Bulk Actions feature allows recruiters to perform actions on multiple candidates simultaneously.

This helps improve efficiency when managing large candidate pools.

---

## Filtering Candidates

Recruiters can refine candidate results using available filters.

### AI Score

Filter candidates based on their AI matching score.

This helps recruiters focus on highly relevant profiles.

---

### Experience

Filter candidates based on years of experience.

Recruiters can narrow results to match job requirements.

---

### Skills

Filter candidates by specific technical or functional skills.

Example:

* Java
* Spring Boot
* Microservices
* REST APIs

---

### Location

Filter candidates based on geographic location.

This helps identify candidates within preferred hiring regions.

---

### Modified Date

Candidates can be sorted by profile modification date.

Available options:

* Ascending
* Descending

This helps recruiters identify recently updated profiles.

---

## Benefits

Candidate Discovery helps recruiters:

* Identify relevant candidates quickly
* Reduce manual sourcing effort
* Prioritize candidates using AI scores
* Improve screening efficiency
* Build recruitment pipelines faster
* Accelerate hiring decisions
