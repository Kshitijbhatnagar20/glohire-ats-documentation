# Dashboard Overview

## Purpose

The Dashboard serves as the central workspace of Glohire ATS. It provides users with a consolidated view of recruitment activities, hiring metrics, job requirements, candidate movement, and operational tasks across the selected Business Unit.

After logging in and selecting a Business Unit, users are directed to the Dashboard where they can monitor recruitment performance and navigate to various ATS modules.

---

## Intended Users

The Dashboard is accessible to:

* Account Manager
* Delivery Manager
* Technical Recruiter

The information displayed is based on the user's access permissions and the selected Business Unit.

---

## Accessing the Dashboard

### Navigation Flow

```text
Login
    ↓
Select Business Unit
    ↓
Dashboard
```

The Dashboard loads recruitment information associated with the selected client or Business Unit.

---

## Dashboard Components

The Dashboard provides quick visibility into recruitment operations and hiring progress.

Key information available on the Dashboard includes:

* Open Job Requirements
* Active Recruitment Activities
* Candidate Status Tracking
* Hiring Progress Monitoring
* Recruitment Performance Metrics
* Recent Operational Activities

---

## Recruitment Monitoring

The Dashboard enables users to monitor ongoing recruitment activities without navigating into individual modules.

Users can quickly identify:

* Active jobs
* Candidate movement across stages
* Submission activities
* Hiring progress
* Recruitment workload

---

## Business Unit Context

All Dashboard information is displayed within the context of the selected Business Unit.

Users can switch Business Units to view recruitment activities associated with a different client.

---

## Navigation to Other Modules

The Dashboard serves as the entry point to major ATS modules, including:

* Jobs
* Candidates
* Pipeline
* Submissions
* Placements
* Client Management
* Vendor Management

---

## Benefits

The Dashboard helps users:

* Monitor recruitment activities from a single location
* Track hiring progress efficiently
* Access operational information quickly
* Navigate to ATS modules with minimal effort
* Improve visibility into recruitment performance
